Explore Bangladesh — Project Overview

1. Project Introduction

Explore Bangladesh is a tourism and travel management platform designed to help people discover beautiful places across Bangladesh, plan trips, share travel experiences, and connect with local tour guides, hotels, and restaurants.

The main goal is to make traveling easier, more informative, and more convenient for both local and international tourists.

2. How Does It Help?

- Easy destination discovery: Tourists can explore popular destinations, tourist attractions, and districts across Bangladesh.
- Travel experience sharing: Travelers can share photos, stories, travel costs, and helpful tips.
- District tracking: Travelers can track which districts they have visited and which places they want to explore next.
- Cost transparency: Tour guides, hotels, and restaurants can share their prices so tourists can estimate their travel budgets.
- Better travel planning: Users can discover destinations, guides, accommodation, and food options in one platform.
- Trusted information: Reviews and ratings can help travelers make better decisions.

3. Four Types of Users

 1.Traveler

Explores destinations, tracks visited districts, plans trips, shares experiences, and reviews services.

 2. Tour Guide

Shares tour packages, service costs, experience, available destinations, and contact information.

 3. Hotel & Restaurant Owner

Publishes business information, locations, facilities, menus, room prices, and special offers.


 4. Administrator

Manages users, verifies guides and businesses, maintains destination information, and moderates inappropriate content.

4. Initial Development Plan

The first version will use basic HTML to create the homepage, login page, and separate registration forms for the user types. Later, CSS, JavaScript, and a backend with a database can be added to implement interactive maps, user accounts, trip planning, reviews, and booking features.
